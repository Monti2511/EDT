    Tree of life

    The vertebrate branches from your conversations, from jawless fish to birds and placental mammals. Each group sits inside the one above it, so birds are inside dinosaurs and you are inside the lobe-finned fish. Tap a date to see that moment on the timeline. † marks a group with no living members.

      Unit boundaries follow the International Commission on Stratigraphy's chart (2023 edition). Most boundaries are fixed by a "golden spike" (GSSP): one point in one rock section, such as the first appearance of the conodont Hindeodus parvus at Meishan, China, for the base of the Triassic. The Hadean has no formal base and starts here with the oldest solids of the solar system. Event and divergence dates are approximate, and many are still debated. The 24-hour clock squeezes all 4.567 billion years of Earth's history into one day, starting at midnight.
